# Simply Classic Insanity Server Rules

The following are the rules for Simply Classic Insanity. To ensure an enjoyable experience, please read the rules thoroughly.

## NOTE
This server uses many modified versions of addons. Please read the [FAQ](https://github.com/SCI-Revived/.github/blob/main/faq.md)

## General Rules
1. Do not attempt to propkill, propfly, or propblock. These are generally referred to as prop abuse.
2. Do not spam in chat or on the mic.
3. Do not make references to extremist groups, ideologies, or individuals.
4. Do not use cheats or exploits.
5. Do not abuse addons or other features for unintended behaviors that may negatively impact the experience of other players (Ex. E2 Wallhacks, Nocolliding into bases, blatantly overpowered contraptions, etc.)
6. Be respectful to players and staff.
7. Do not attempt to lag or crash the server.
8. Do not derail other people's trains.
9. Do not attempt to circumvent spawnmenu restrictions.
10. Do not attempt to enter the skybox of the map.
11. If a player asks you to stop, you must stop (enforcement is up to staff discretion).
12. RDM is allowed.

## PVP/Base Rules
NOTE: This server uses Simple Prop Damage. Props can only be damaged with explosives.

1. Do not abuse build/pvp mode to gain an unfair advantage in combat (Ex. escaping combat, ambushing players, using buildmode to deliver gbombs, etc.)
2. Do not spam Gbombs or use the physgun to attack players with Gbombs.
3. Do not spawnkill or spawntrap.
4. Do not despawn simfphys/LVS vehicles to escape death.
5. Do not set spawn inside bases without the owner's permission.
6. Do not set spawn in close proximity to a base you are attacking. Leave a reasonable distance between your spawn and the base.
7. All Bases must have an entrance either secured with a keypad door or open. Keypads should open doors for 3 seconds minimum.
8. Do not make 1-way windows.
9. Do not make indestructible bases.
10. Do not occupy large chunks of the map with your base.

## Warning to Players
Application of punishments are up to staff discretion and will match the severity of the violation(s). Arguing like a lawyer over a technicality is not going to save you from punishments if you are negatively affecting the experience of other players (Ex. "But it's not in the rules"). Use of common sense is highly recommended to avoid punishment.
