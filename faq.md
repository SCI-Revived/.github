# Frequently Asked Questions

## Addons

### Wasn't Gbombs 5 backdoored?
We use a customized version of Gbombs 5 with the backdoor removed. The original backdoor was not a virus or ransomware, it just gave the addon's creator admin on any server that had it mounted.

### Why does M9K feel different?
Like Gbombs 5, we use a customized version of the addon. The base M9K addon is horribly balanced and makes only 3-5 guns viable. Our version of M9K intends to resolve this by making all weapons good in the right hands. As a result, it is a matter of player preferences. Assault rifles have high recoil and damage but reward precision shooting, while submachine guns have low damage and recoil but are easier to handle.

### What is Simple Prop Damage?
Simple Prop Damage assigns health to every prop in the server, meaning every prop is destructible. Props can only be damaged via explosives, and this is what Gbombs 5 is for. It is on the server to prevent people from creating indestructible 1-way bases that are impossible to raid conventionally. Props calculate their health based on their volume. The larger the prop, the more health it has. This is capped at 60,000 health.

### What is the point of all this balancing?
Simply Classic Insanity intends to emulate the 2016 Gmod server experience, which had a strong emphasis on a chaotic but overall casual environment with a large combat sandbox. By giving players several solutions to
one problem, casual players do not have to grind on one specific skillset to accomplish their goals (Ex. A tank can be destroyed via aircraft, emplacements, RPGs, other tanks, or gbombs. No one solution is the only option.)

## Server

### How do I rank up?
Like the original SCI server network, promotion is not linear. Players will automatically reach "Proven" after 6 hours of playtime. To be promoted to Senior Proven, a player must have 24 hours of playtime on the server and good behavior. Having a recent history of rule violations will delay promotion.

### Why is everything restricted?
To prevent malicious actors from disrupting the server, the "User" rank has access to only basic features. You can expect to unlock more features upon ranking up.
